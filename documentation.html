Documentation

Version 2.0

CAVES is two things: a standalone app, and an AUv3 instrument you can play in GarageBand. Both use the same sound engine, so a sound made in one sounds the same in the other. Most controls are the same in both; the differences are noted below.

Using CAVES in GarageBand (AUv3)

LOADING: Install the CAVES app, then add a new track in GarageBand and choose CAVES from its list of Audio Unit instruments
PLAYING: Play notes from GarageBand's keyboard, a MIDI keyboard or a recorded region. Oscillator 1 plays the note's pitch and Oscillator 2 follows it at its own ratio; key velocity sets the level
OCT: Moves Oscillator 1 up or down by up to 3 octaves. The FREQ knob is greyed out, because the played note sets the pitch
TEMPO: Synced LFOs and envelopes follow the song's tempo and bar position
PRESETS: "CAVES Default" is built in. Save your own sounds with GarageBand's preset menu; your project also remembers every setting. Patches saved in the CAVES app are not available in GarageBand yet
AUTOMATION: Every knob and switch can be automated in GarageBand for Mac (GarageBand for iPad and iPhone can't automate instrument plug-ins)
TABS: The plug-in window has the same VCO, VCF, ENV, LFO and FX sections as the app, with shorter labels (AMP for LEVEL, RES for RESONANCE, FB for FEEDBACK, WET for DRY/WET)

Signal Routing

CAVES uses a straightforward signal path designed for rich, evolving drone sounds:

OSC1 (PWM) + OSC2 (FM)
↓
FILTERS (LP + LADDER with routing)
↓
DISTORTION
↓
DELAY
↓
REVERB
↓
MASTER VOLUME → OUTPUT

Modulation sources (LFOs and Envelopes) can be routed to 14 parameters throughout the signal chain for dynamic, evolving sounds.

The output stays clean up to just below full scale, then rounds off gently instead of clipping harshly, so a runaway delay or a screaming filter never goes over 0 dB.

Tips

DOUBLE-TAP: Double-tap any knob in the plug-in to reset it to its default
VOICEOVER: In the plug-in, every knob speaks its name and value; swipe up or down to adjust it

VCO (Oscillators)

Oscillator 1 (PWM)

FREQ: Oscillator frequency (10-440 Hz). App only; in GarageBand the played note sets the pitch
OCT: Octave, -3 to +3 (plug-in only)
LEVEL: Output amplitude (0-100%)
PULSE WIDTH: Pulse width for harmonic content

Oscillator 2 (FM)

FREQ: Base frequency (20-440 Hz). In GarageBand it follows the played note
LEVEL: Output amplitude (0-100%)
FM INDEX: FM modulation depth (0-10)
RATIO: Carrier multiplier for harmonic ratios

Master Controls

MASTER LEVEL: Overall output level (post-effects)

VCF (Filters)

Filter 1 (Low Pass)

CUTOFF: Filter cutoff frequency (50-8000 Hz)
RESONANCE: Filter resonance amount

Filter 2 (Ladder)

CUTOFF: Ladder filter cutoff frequency (50-8000 Hz)
RESONANCE: Ladder filter resonance amount

Routing

OSC → FILTER: Route oscillators to filter inputs. An oscillator that isn't routed to a filter is silent

ENV (Envelopes)

Envelope 1 & 2

ATTACK: Rise time to peak level (0.1-300 s)
DECAY: Fall time to sustain level (0.1-300 s)
SUSTAIN: Level held between decay and release
RELEASE: Fall time to zero (0.1-600 s)
AMOUNT: How far the envelope moves its target, from -100% (inverted) to +100%
TARGET: Parameter to modulate. Choose "Off" to turn the envelope off; choosing a target turns it on
MODE: One Shot (runs once) or Looping (repeats)
SYNC: "Free", or a clock division (8 bars to 1/32) to restart the envelope on the beat. A synced envelope runs once per division, even in Looping mode
TRIGGER: Starts the envelope by hand (TRIG in the plug-in). Each new note also restarts it, from its current level
MASTER LEVEL TARGET: An envelope on Master Level acts as the amplifier: the sound is silent until the envelope opens it

LFO (Low Frequency Oscillators)

LFO 1 & 2

RATE: LFO speed: 0.001-0.5 Hz in SLOW, 0.01-10 Hz in FAST
SLOW/FAST: Rate range (very slow or normal speeds)
SYNC: Locks the LFO to the tempo; the DIVISION knob (the RATE knob in the plug-in) then picks a note length from 8 bars to 1/32
DEPTH: Modulation amount
WAVEFORM: Sine, triangle, square, sawtooth, random
TARGET: Parameter to modulate. Choose "Off" to turn the LFO off; choosing a target turns it on

FX (Effects)

Distortion

DRIVE: Distortion intensity (-20 to +20 dB)
DRY/WET: Dry/wet balance

Delay

TIME: Delay time (up to 2 seconds). Changing it while echoes ring bends their pitch, like tape
FEEDBACK: Delay regeneration amount. High settings with a held note can swell and build up
DRY/WET: Dry/wet balance; set to zero for no delay

Reverb

ENABLED: Turn reverb on/off (ON in the plug-in)
DRY/WET: Reverb amount
SIZE: From a small room (the CAVES v1 sound) to a long hall

Transport (App)

START/STOP: Starts and stops the drone and the clock. Synced LFOs and envelopes follow the patch's tempo (120 BPM in CAVES Default)
RESET: Reset all parameters to the patch as it was loaded

Patch Management (App)

CAVES includes a complete patch system for saving and organizing your sounds. Patches save all oscillator & filter settings, modulation, effects, and clock settings.

Saving Patches

SAVE BUTTON: Tap the save icon (top right, next to settings) to save current sound
PATCH NAME: Enter custom name or use the auto name generator
AUTO NAME: Automagically generate names for your patches

Loading Patches

PATCH BROWSER: Tap the current patch name to browse saved patches
TAP TO LOAD: Simply tap any patch name to load it immediately

Deleting Patches

SWIPE TO DELETE: Swipe left on any user patch to delete it

Patch Sharing

SHARE CURRENT PATCH: Email patches to the developer from Settings > Community
PATCH FILES: Patches are sent as .drone files with metadata

Patches From CAVES 1.x

Version 2.0 has a new sound engine with smoother modulation. The first time it starts, CAVES converts your saved patches and adjusts their modulation depths to sound as close to before as possible. Most patches sound about the same; patches that modulate Master Level may sound different, because an envelope on Master Level now works as the amplifier. Your original patch files are kept.
